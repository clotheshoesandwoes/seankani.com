Our birria

Eight hours
before you order.

The birria starts the night before. Chiles toasted by hand, beef shoulder braised low until it pulls apart with a look. By the time we open, the consomé has been reducing for hours.

We learned it from abuela's kitchen in Jalisco and we haven't changed much. No shortcuts, no powdered anything. Just the brasa, the chiles, and a lot of patience.
