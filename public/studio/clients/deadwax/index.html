Sell us your records.

We buy collections, big and small. Vinyl from the '60s on, plus CDs and tapes in good shape. A little of everything: rock, jazz, soul, hip-hop, punk, reggae, and more.

  1. Tell us what you've got

    Rough count, the genres, the condition, and a few photos if you can.

  2. We take a look

    We get back within a day or two with an offer, or a time to come see it in person.

  3. Get paid

    Bring it by and walk out with cash, or more store credit if you'd rather trade up.

We're a little selective, so not every collection is a fit. No water-damaged records, please. No hard feelings either way.
