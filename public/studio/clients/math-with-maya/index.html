Try it yourself

A lesson, in your hands.

This is roughly how a session feels. Drag a, b, c to reshape the parabola, then drag the teal dot along the curve and watch the tangent line tilt. Its steepness is the derivative. Flip on the area button to see the integral fill in.

When the discriminant drops below zero, the curve lifts off the x-axis and the real roots disappear. Watching that happen tends to stick better than memorizing the rule for it.
